# Company Overview

Northstar Analytics is a fictional B2B software company that helps operations teams monitor supply chain performance.
Its platform combines inventory health metrics, demand forecasts, and supplier issue alerts in one dashboard.
The company sells annual subscriptions to mid-market manufacturers and logistics providers.

## Core Use Cases

- Monitor supplier risk and delivery delays.
- Identify inventory shortages before they impact customers.
- Investigate root causes behind demand forecast misses.
