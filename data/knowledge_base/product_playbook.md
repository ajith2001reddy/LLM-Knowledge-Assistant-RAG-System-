# Product Playbook

Northstar Analytics offers three plans:

- Starter: dashboard access for one business unit and weekly reporting.
- Growth: multi-team dashboards, custom alerts, and API access.
- Enterprise: SSO, advanced analytics, dedicated support, and custom onboarding.

## Implementation Notes

Typical deployments take two to four weeks.
The product integrates with ERP, WMS, and CSV-based operational exports.
Customers usually start with inventory and supplier data before adding demand planning workflows.
